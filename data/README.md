This folder contains the graphical results and data analysis comparing experimental measurements with theoretical models and simulations.

## Experimental Analysis & Models

*   **darcy.png**  
    Validation of the **Darcy Law** (linear regime) applied to our experimental data.
*   **ergun.png**  
    Application of the **Ergun Equation** (quadratic regime) applied to our experimental data.
*   **residus_darcy.png** & **residus_ergun.png**  
    Error analysis (residuals) for both models to evaluate their fit accuracy.

## System Characteristics

*   **caracteristique_debit_tension.png**  
    Calibration curve showing the relationship between the fan's supply voltage and the resulting air flow rate.
*   **composante_de_surpression.png**  
    Analysis of the pressure drop specifically caused by the **porous support** on which the sand bed rests.

## Simulation vs. Reality

*   **comparaison_simulation_experience.png**  
    **The key validation plot.** It compares the minimum fluidization velocity ($V_{mf}$) obtained experimentally versus the DEM simulation results for various materials: **Sand, Salt, Quinoa, Chia seeds, and Lentils.**
